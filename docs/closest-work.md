# Closest-work comparison and justified delta

The question is not whether affine counting is more expressive than general
certified counting: it is not. The exact fault-token translation gives a
parsimonious general Boolean encoding. The limited research increment is
eliminating unchanged-fragment queries under a fully verified exact-coverage
invariant, including policy changes, strict history preservation and canonical
least-fault diagnostics. The following comparisons refer to primary records
and technical passages in `references.json`; they are not external peer review.

| Work family | What the source provides | Consequence for this manuscript |

## Completed comparison evidence

The manuscript contains a claim-specific comparison, a proof that the exact
application defect equals the model count of a deterministic-auxiliary CNF,
finite checks of the encoder, exported full and reduced CNFs, and measured
sizes for the identical 20 inputs. The native generic baseline is unmodified
Z3 4.13.3.0 on full/reduced QF_LIA safety formulas, with raw timeouts and model
checks. This is a separately labelled trusted decision experiment.

## Uncompleted empirical comparison

The complete upstream CPOG/d4+certifier+formal checker or VeriPB/CakePB pipeline
was not acquired and executed. Tool/repository metadata was inspected; attempts
to obtain directly executable/materializable dependencies did not yield a
working pipeline. No result from a locally named imitation is substituted.
There is no measured performance win against the strongest general certified
counting implementation. The current evidence supports compatibility and
specialization, not general proof-size or runtime dominance. An end-to-end
comparison including frontend construction, proof generation and proof checking
remains a substantive limitation of the research evidence.
